# Demo Flow

## Goal

Show Electron moving from anomaly to field action without claiming unverified theft.

## Planned Demo Sequence

1. Open dashboard and show active grid health.
2. Open Digital Twin.
3. Inject a scenario such as coordinated theft or meter malfunction.
4. Show telemetry entering the normal pipeline.
5. Open consumer analysis with personal baseline and peer comparison.
6. Show transformer unexplained loss correlation.
7. Open investigation case.
8. Review evidence, alternatives, and checklist.
9. Use voice copilot concept to ask why the consumer was flagged.
10. Add field observation.
11. Resolve case and show feedback loop.
12. Open stress-test view with computed metrics only if a real run exists.

## Demo Constraint

Do not present fake metrics, fake integrations, or fake field confirmations.
